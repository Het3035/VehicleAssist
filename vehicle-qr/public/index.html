Questions people ask

Can the person who scans see my phone number?

No. They see the last four characters of your plate and any note you leave. Your number is shown only if you turn on the call button for that vehicle.

Does the person scanning need an account?

No. They scan or type the plate, pick a reason and send. They only share a phone number if they choose to type one.

What if someone spams my sticker?

Each sticker accepts a few alerts every ten minutes. You can dismiss any message, and you can delete a vehicle to stop its sticker working.

How do I get alerts on my phone?

Open your dashboard, choose Enable phone notifications, and allow them in your browser. Alerts also always appear in your dashboard.

Does it cost anything?

No. It is free to use, with no ads and no tracking.
